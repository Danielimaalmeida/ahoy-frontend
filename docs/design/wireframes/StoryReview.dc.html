StoryPROJ · BillingReady to create Charge late fees on overdue Enterprise invoices
User story As finance, I want overdue Enterprise invoices to carry a late fee that I approve, so that customers pay on time and we stop chasing them by hand.
Acceptance criteria
AC1Only accounts on the Enterprise plan can get a late fee.
AC2An invoice 30 days past its due date is proposed for a fee of 1.5% of the overdue amount per month.
AC3Finance approves or rejects each proposed fee before it is added to the invoice.
AC4Total fees on one invoice never exceed 10% of it.
AC5The fee shows as its own line on the invoice PDF.
Out of scope Contract accounts. Waiving a fee after it is charged.
